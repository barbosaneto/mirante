# Governance

Mirante uses a maintainer-led governance model while the project is in its
experimental `0.x` phase.

## Roles

- **Users** run or customize Mirante and provide feedback.
- **Contributors** submit issues, documentation, tests, code, reviews, or
  translations.
- **Maintainers** review changes, manage releases and repository settings,
  respond to security and conduct reports, and protect the project's scope and
  architectural boundaries.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive participation. Maintainers
may invite contributors to take on additional responsibility based on the
quality, consistency, and breadth of their work.

## Decisions

Routine decisions are made through issue and pull request review. Significant
changes to package boundaries, public extension contracts, compatibility,
security, licensing, or governance should begin with an issue and be recorded
in an architecture decision record when accepted.

Maintainers seek practical consensus. When consensus cannot be reached, the
maintainers responsible for the affected area make the decision and document
the reasoning and tradeoffs.

## Releases

Maintainers decide when a revision is ready for release, assign versions under
the documented semantic-versioning policy, maintain the changelog, and publish
artifacts. No individual contribution guarantees a release date.

## Independence

Mirante is an independent community project. It is not governed by, affiliated
with, or endorsed by GeoNode, OSGeo, or GeoSolutions. References to those
projects describe interoperability and upstream dependencies only.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require a public pull request with a clear rationale and an
appropriate review period. Security or conduct details that must remain private
are handled under their respective policies.
